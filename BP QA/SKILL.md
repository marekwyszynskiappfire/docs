# BP QA–Development Cooperation Process

This workstream starts without inherited Ways of Working agreements.

## Source policy

- Treat decisions captured in this working session as **Draft**. They become **Agreed** only after review by the necessary stakeholders.
- Label proposals, assumptions, and unresolved topics clearly.
- Do not infer policy from archived BP QA files.
- Verify current Trinity behavior from `Trinity/` documentation.
- Verify Legion details from [Legion](https://docs-dts.appfire.net/platform-documentation/qa-coe/legion/) and this page. Do not invent extra Legion behaviour.
- Never invent Jira, Confluence, or tool links.

## Document workflow

1. Gather draft decisions and open questions by topic.
2. Separate current practice from the intended future process.
3. State ownership and decision rights explicitly.
4. Record each unresolved item with an owner or required decision.
5. Produce one succinct, skimmable Confluence page; link detailed tool
   documentation rather than duplicating it.

## Required coverage

- Trinity
- Legion
- epic acceptance testing
- QA involvement
- test automation and maintenance
- capacity management
- active initiatives
- agreements and loose ends

Do not modify Trinity implementation as part of documenting this process.
